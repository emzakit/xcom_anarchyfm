========================================
 Anarchy Radio FM  —  Resistance Radio, your way
========================================

Anarchy Radio FM lets you replace XCOM 2's music with your OWN local audio files, played
in sync with the game — menu music on the menu, base music on the Avenger,
something loud when the shooting starts.

This is an update to my original Resistance Radio mod, it allows you to replace
the ingame soundtrack with local files or Spotify (experimental, but it works).

This Workshop item is the tiny IN-GAME half. It watches the game's screens and
quietly reports what's happening so the companion Anarchy Radio FM desktop app can play the
right tracks from folders on your PC. On its own it does nothing audible — you
need the desktop app too (see below).

----------------------------------------
 REQUIRED
----------------------------------------
1. Music Modding System (MMS) — enabled in your mod list. Anarchy Radio FM is an ADD-ON to
   MMS, not a replacement; it leans on MMS to silence the game's own music:
       https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=757398474
2. The Anarchy Radio FM desktop app — the external player that actually plays your music.
   Get it (and the full setup guide) here:
       https://github.com/emzakit/xcom_anarchyfm

----------------------------------------
 CHANGING "STATIONS" (the hacky way)
----------------------------------------
There's no on-screen menu, that caused an annoying amount of issues to make so I stripped it out (for now). To shuffle to something
new, just dip IN AND OUT OF THE GEOSCAPE and back, exactly like the original
Resistance Radio mod.

Each time you come back it reshuffles, and with Radio Mode
switched on in the desktop app it even drops you in at a new random spot — like
retuning to a fresh station.

Prefer buttons? Open the Mod Config Menu (MCM) → Anarchy Radio FM → the
"Playback Controls" group has Play / Pause / Next / Back buttons that control
the desktop player straight away.

Or from the console, these commands do the same (bind them to keys if you like):
    XiPodPlay    XiPodPause    XiPodNext    XiPodPrev

----------------------------------------
 GOOD TO KNOW
----------------------------------------
- The Avenger and Squad Select experience is the polished, intended part.
- Combat / tactical states are experimental and may be a bit rough — that's
  what MMS music packs are great for, and Anarchy Radio FM is happy to run alongside them.
- Everything is configured in the desktop app; nothing here needs tweaking.
