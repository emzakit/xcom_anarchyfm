[h1]Anarchy Radio FM[/h1]

[b]Your music. Your XCOM. Finally.[/b]

Drop in .mp3, .ogg, .wav, .flac, .m4a or .opus and it plays in sync with the game. No scary gigantic .upk files, no conversions, no ffmpeg. Files in folders. That's the whole idea.

Hans Zimmer scoring your Geoscape while the world quietly falls apart? Go on then. Slipknot the second the shooting starts? Absolutely. The Spice Girls over an ADVENT ambush because you thought it'd be funny and now it's canon? Nobody here is going to stop you.

[b]Watch it in action:[/b] https://youtu.be/y4coRhi1n3w

[hr][/hr]
[h2]⚠ THREE THINGS, OR IT WON'T MAKE A SOUND[/h2]

[b]This Workshop item is the in-game half and makes exactly zero noise on its own.[/b] All it does is watch your screens and quietly report what's happening. The actual music comes from a companion desktop app.

[list]
[*] [b]1.[/b] Subscribe to this [b]and[/b] to [url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=757398474]Music Modding System[/url]. MMS does the silencing. Nothing here works without it.
[*] [b]2.[/b] Add [b]-forcelogflush[/b] to XCOM 2's launch options. In Steam: right-click XCOM 2 → Properties → Launch Options. Skip this one and music plays straight over your cinematics.
[*] [b]3.[/b] [url=https://github.com/emzakit/xcom_anarchyfm/releases]Grab the desktop app[/url], unzip it anywhere, run the exe. A wizard handles the rest.
[/list]

Then drop your music into the folders it makes for you. Done.

[i]Don't trust a random exe off the internet? Good instinct. It's all open source — [/i][url=https://github.com/emzakit/xcom_anarchyfm/wiki/Building-the-exe]build it yourself[/url][i].[/i]

[h2]📻 Radio Mode — the good bit[/h2]

One button. The Avenger tunes into a station and [b]every track starts at a random point[/b] — not from the top, from wherever the broadcast happens to be. It never feels like a playlist looping round. It feels like a signal that was already running before you walked in.

[b]The tip that'll sell you on it instantly:[/b] grab a few hour-long GTA radio station rips — DJ banter, fake adverts, the lot — and drop them in. Every trip back to the ship lands you mid-song, mid-advert or mid-ramble. It's ridiculous and it's the best thing here.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]There's plenty more[/h2]

Effects (want the Avenger sounding like a beaten-up field radio? two clicks), Workshop music packs that just turn up in your library, per-screen volume and looping, Spotify if you're feeling brave, and it updates itself.

[b]Already running an MMS music pack?[/b] Grand. Fill the folders you care about and the pack quietly covers everything else.
